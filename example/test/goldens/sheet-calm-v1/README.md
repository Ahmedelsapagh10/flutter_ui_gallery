# Sheet Calm v1

40 individually inspected pinned-renderer expectations: 12 versioned existing
Orbit Notes specimens 24 new Orbit/SplitLayer context/content/action views, and four versioned Wallet
SplitLayer views.
All historical images and approval manifests remain unchanged. Exact mappings,
SHA-256 hashes and renderer/source provenance are in `provenance.json`.

Normal and 2× phone layouts retain a footer outside the content scroller. At
extreme text plus keyboard constraints the whole sheet scrolls to keep header,
form and footer reachable. Caller-controlled self-scrolling content stays bounded.
The asymmetric Orbit silhouette and distinct family edges remain intact.
